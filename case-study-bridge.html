Bridge Global — International Student Onboarding Platform

UX/UI Research & Requirements Architecture | UCF Project

Business Problem & User Need

International students transitioning to UCF often navigate fragmented tools across visa compliance, mentor matching, and scholarship resources. The goal of Bridge Global was to consolidate these disparate workflows into a single centralized onboarding hub.

UX Research & Requirement Engineering

  • Stakeholder & User Research: Conducted user research to identify pain points faced by international students during orientation and onboarding.
  • User Personas & Journey Mapping: Formulated detailed personas and mapping flows to guide interface hierarchy and feature prioritization.
  • Prototyping & System Design: Designed low-fidelity wireframes and high-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma.
  • Functional Integration: Specified functional requirements for four primary modules: visa guidance, mentor matching, scholarship discovery, and orientation resources.

Technical Implementation

Design & Research: Figma, Wireframing, User Personas | Frontend Development: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript

Business Impact & Deliverables

  • Streamlined student onboarding by centralizing four fragmented administrative services into a unified user interface.
  • Delivered a fully interactive prototype and web-based platform ready for user testing and deployment.
